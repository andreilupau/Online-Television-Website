Avalanche danger at high altitudes. Mountain rescuers warn

Fresh snow and unstable layers raise the risk of slab avalanches in popular alpine routes this weekend.

By Digi24 (Editor: B.P.)•Nov 14, 2025•3 min read

Romanian mountain rescuers are warning hikers and ski tourers about a higher avalanche risk at altitude, after fresh snowfall combined with strong wind created unstable “wind slabs” on exposed slopes.

What changed in the last days

According to rescuers, it snowed significantly above roughly 1,800 meters, while gusts strengthened enough to transport snow and compact it into plates. When these layers break, they can trigger avalanches—sometimes even when the release is relatively small.

Weekend warning

The advisory mentions very strong winds (over 100 km/h in exposed areas) and a sharp drop in temperatures, with values around −16°C near high peaks like Vârful Omu. In such conditions, stability can change quickly from one ridge or valley to the next.

Practical advice

Rescuers recommend avoiding steep couloirs and high‑altitude routes, spacing out when crossing exposed sections, and sticking to safer, lower trails. They also remind hikers that many marked high‑mountain routes are officially closed during winter.

“If you hear collapsing sounds or see cracks, turn back—conditions can change in minutes.”
