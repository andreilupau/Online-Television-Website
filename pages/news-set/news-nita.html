The diagnosis Florin Niță received after the injury in the match in Cyprus

Medical scans offer a clearer timeline for recovery, easing concern among national team supporters.

By a1.ro (Redacție a1.ro)•Nov 14, 2025•2 min read

Florin Niță picked up an injury during Romania’s match against Cyprus after an intense sequence in which he prevented an equalizer. Shortly afterward, he signaled discomfort and was replaced around the 67th minute.

Initial diagnosis

The first information circulated in Romanian sports media described the issue as a muscle injury, with further medical evaluation needed to confirm severity and recovery time.

What it means for selection

The incident reopened the discussion about depth at goalkeeper, with the coaching staff expected to rely on available alternatives if rest is required. Updates typically depend on follow‑up scans and clinical response in the days after the match.

“Early assessments matter, but final timelines depend on follow‑up checks and how the injury responds.”
